# AGILE MINDSET 

## AGILE Manifesto 

- consists of 4 values and 12 principles 

### The Four Values 
1. Value individuals and interactions over process and tools
2. Working Software over comprehensive documentation 
3. Customer collaboration over contract negotiation i.o.w be more flexible and accomodating <br/> (since agile has changes so we need to accomadate for them)
4. Responding to change over following a plan

### The 12 Principles 
1. The highest priority is to satisfy the customer through early and cts delivery of valuable software 
2. Welcome the changing requirements regardless of the stage or phase of the project. Agile process harness change for the customer's competitive advantage 
3. Deliver working software frequently with a preference to the shorter timescale 
4. Business people and developers (technical) must forward together daily throughout the project
5. Build the projects around motivated individuals - give them the environment and support needed and trust them 
6. The most efficient and effective method of conveying info to and within a development team is F2F communication 
7. Working software is the primary measure of progress 
8. Agile process promote sustainable development - stakeholders should be able to maintain a constant pace indefinitely 
9. Cts attention to technical excellence and good design enhances agility 
10. Simplicity - optimize the amount of work done 
11. The best architecture, requirements, and design emerge from self organizing 
12. At regular intervals, the team reflects on how to become more effective and adjust its behavior accordingly 

## 2. Agile Methodologies 

Most team will use a combination of different methodologies. 

### 2.1 Scrum 

Scrum has three main pillars:
1. Transparency <br/> work must be visible to all the key players
2. Inspection <br/> work towards the agreed goals must be checked frequently to determine problems 
3. Adaption <br/>  learn how to adjust with unexpected situations 
<hr/>
The five scrum values <br/>

1. Commitment <br/> 
2. Focus <br/>
3. Openess <br/>
4. Respect <br/>
5. Courage
<hr/>

 The key roles within scrum are the following: <br/>

1. Product Owner (PO) <br/>

-  The business representative responsible for optimizing the value from the resulting work 
- responsible for managing the product backlog 
- ensure the business and the team have the shared vision for the project 

2. Scrum Master <br/>
- A servant leader but unlike PM they ensure that the scrum methods are being used correctly 
- coaches the team to be self managing and cross functional
- removes any road block to achieve scrum 

3. Developers <br/>
- no sub team or hierarchies 
- self managing, they internally decide who does what, when, and how 
- typically 10 or fewer
