## PMI ACP Notes 

**1. A team has not addressed the strict data privacy requirement and is planning to do at the end of development. What should an agile PM do?**

Answer → They should work with the PO to integrate the information into the product backlog and deliver it incrementally 

### Why?
- Only the PO (product owner) can add to the backlog 

**2. A developer points out that a certain feature may or may not be available on time and may cause downstream. What should an Agile PM do?**

Answer → Identify it as a risk and assess the impact. Then plan mitgation strategies during release planning. 

### Why?
- The keyword is may or may not affect 
- If the choice is related to giving away the problem or situation to someone, it is less likely the choice 

**3. How can a PM solve the growing misalignment between several stakeholders and project members due to the stakeloders missing scheduled meetings?**

Answer → Use information radiators 

### Why?
- Information radiators ensure visibility and open communication by making updates and status available to all the stakeholders 
- Examples are using Kanban, Project Board, etc. 
- Increasing meetings is not efficient or assigning a liason
